Dear CIS Advisory team,

Following the Information Security Risk Acceptance Procedure (ISRAP), and on the advice of Mahmoud Abdullah (Network Security), I am submitting an ISRA for a Knauf Israel application.

What it is
knauf-il-leads.com is a small web application owned and operated by Knauf Israel. Our sales representatives use it to capture leads at trade conferences (name, phone, e-mail, company, role, area of interest), assign each lead to a representative and follow it up until it is entered into the CRM. It is the first stage of the lead process, before CRM entry. About 19 users; a few hundred leads per year.

Why an ISRA
The site is blocked on Knauf-managed devices by the web access policy (Zscaler, "Professional Services" category), so representatives cannot do the follow-up work from their Knauf laptops. Mahmoud has approved the URL access itself subject to your assessment, since the application collects customer contact data (PII).

Key facts for your assessment
- Hosting: Supabase (database, authentication, server functions) in AWS eu-central-1 Frankfurt; Netlify serves only the static front-end files. No connection to any Knauf system.
- Access: every screen requires sign-in with a one-time code to the user's Knauf mailbox; only pre-registered representatives can sign in; no passwords stored; authorisation enforced in the database (row-level security).
- Data: B2B business contact details only; no special-category data. Privacy notice on the form with the Knauf privacy policy link and a named privacy contact (Keren Shay).
- Retention: leads are deleted automatically 24 months after the last contact; backups roll off within 30 days. Marketing consent is collected separately (opt-in).
- E-mail: the system notifies Knauf Israel representatives only (internal recipients, no links). It never e-mails the lead/customer; customer contact is done by the representative, and once a lead is entered into the Knauf CRM, the CRM is the system of record.
- Sub-processors: Supabase and Netlify under their DPAs (attached). The outbound notification mailbox is currently a Gmail account without a DPA — this is the one open point, and we would welcome your guidance on moving it to a Knauf mailbox / SMTP relay.
- Risk rating proposed: Low. Business-unit signature is in section 10 of the ISRAD.
